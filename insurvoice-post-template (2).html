4:5 post & carousel builder

Generated images sit underneath. Type directly on any slide to edit it. Export at full 1080 × 1350.

Typing switches the dropdown to Custom. Keep it under ~30 characters.

How to use it
  • Click any text on a slide and type — it edits in place.
  • Click a slide to make it the one that “Download slide” exports.
  • Export renders at 1080 × 1350 px, ready for Instagram.
  • Inner slides stay flat dark on purpose — photography competes with the text.
  • Swap Inter for the real brand face once you have the licence.
